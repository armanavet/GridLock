How to play

Submit

Green — correct positionRight letter in the right place.

Gray — not in the wordThis letter is not in the answer.

A five-letter word is hidden. Your goal is to arrange the letters in the brass window across the middle of the board to form a word, then submit your guess.

You can slide columns up or down and rows left or right. Letters wrap around when moved, and the extra slots at either end of the window allow letters to slide through.

Each column contains a fixed set of letters. If the letter you need isn't in a column, you can slide the rows horizontally to bring it into place.

After submitting a guess, the letters are highlighted with colors:

Green: The letter is correct and in the right position.
Yellow: The letter is in the word but in the wrong position.
Gray: The letter is not in the word.

You have six attempts to guess the hidden word. You can slide letters as many times as you want without losing an attempt. Only submitting a guess counts as an attempt.
